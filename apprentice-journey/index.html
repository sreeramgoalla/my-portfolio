Introduction

In Australia, many apprentices are employed by a Group Training Organisation (GTO), which recruits them, employs them and places them with a host employer. Separately, an apprenticeship support network provider signs up the training contract, registers it with the government and manages incentives and progress check-ins. Two organisations and two platforms serve the same apprentice.

🏗️

Group training platform

Used by the GTO to recruit candidates, place them with host employers, run field visits on mobile, track safety, pay apprentices and invoice hosts.

Used by: GTO recruiters, field officers, payroll and finance

📋

Apprenticeship support platform

Used by the support network provider to sign up training contracts, sync registrations with the government system, schedule progress contacts and process incentive claims.

Used by: provider field and monitoring officers, plus employer, training-provider and apprentice portals

🔗
Two systems, one apprentice. The platforms are separate systems. The handoff between them happens through the training contract: the GTO hires, the support provider signs up and registers, and both then support the apprentice through to completion.

The people in the journey

Five parties, each with a different goal. The journey only works when all five are served.

Jordan, the apprentice

17, finishing school, wants an electrical apprenticeship. Needs a job, a clear path and someone checking in.

Illustrative persona

The GTO

Jordan's legal employer. Recruits, places, pays and supports apprentices, and carries the safety obligations.

The host employer

A local electrical contractor. Supervises Jordan on site and pays the GTO a charge-out rate.

The support provider

Government-contracted to sign up and register training contracts, check in on progress and process incentives.

Government & training authority

Approves the training contract, holds the official registration and pays incentives.

Stage by stage

What happens at each stage, what matters most to the people involved, and how each platform helps.

1

Attract Group training platform

A host employer asks the GTO for an electrical apprentice. The GTO creates a vacancy from a template, gets it approved, and publishes it to job boards and its own careers page. Jordan finds it and applies online.

Moment that mattersJordan's first impression: a simple application that works on a phone.
How the platform helpsVacancy templates, approval workflow and job-board publishing from one record.
2

Assess & select Group training platform

Applications move through a status lifecycle. The GTO screens applicants, runs aptitude assessments, schedules interviews from the vacancy, and markets strong candidates to other hosts through reverse marketing.

Moment that mattersFast feedback. Good candidates drop out when they wait.
How the platform helpsBulk status changes, interview scheduling and potential-candidate matching on the vacancy.
3

Place Group training platform

Jordan accepts. The GTO creates a placement that links Jordan to the host, with host and field details, contract dates, induction and a safety (WHS) record, and assigns a field officer. An action plan schedules the first events and reminders.

Moment that mattersA safe first day: induction done and the right supervisor named.
How the platform helpsPlacement record as the single source of truth; action plans with a preview before they're applied.
4

Sign up Apprenticeship support platform Handoff

The support provider takes over the paperwork. A sign-up moves from lead, through information gathering, to signing by the employer and apprentice. It's then lodged with the state training authority and, once approved, the registration is created in the government system and synced back.

Lead→Sign-up→Signed→Lodged→Approved→Registered
Moment that mattersGetting the contract right first time. A returned contract delays incentives and support.
How the platform helpsA guided sign-up wizard, and registrations that sync from the government system instead of being keyed by hand.
5

Support & monitor Group training Apprenticeship support

Both organisations check in, for different reasons. The GTO's field officer visits Jordan on site on a set schedule, filling in forms on mobile that save even with patchy reception, and logs any safety incidents. The support provider runs progress contacts at fixed milestones, with mentoring where it's needed.

Sign-up7 weeks3 months7 months18 months24 months36 monthsCompletion
Moment that mattersThe early months, when timely support makes the biggest difference.
How the platforms helpVisit schedules with bulk officer assignment; progress contacts created automatically for every registration.
6

Pay & claim Group training Apprenticeship support

The GTO pays Jordan through payroll, invoices the host employer, and tracks its own claims against the apprenticeship. The support provider assesses and processes government incentive claims, such as commencement and completion incentives and the living-away-from-home allowance, with due dates calculated from the registration.

Moment that mattersEmployers only stay in the system if incentives arrive on time.
How the platforms helpInvoices and claims on the placement; claim forecasting and an employer self-service portal.
7

Complete Group training Apprenticeship support

Jordan finishes the qualification. The support provider runs the completion contact and completion claim, the government records the registration as completed, and the GTO records the outcome and awards. The host may offer Jordan ongoing work.

Moment that mattersA clean finish: qualification, final incentive and a job.
How the platforms helpCompletion recorded in both systems, with awards and certificates on the candidate record.

Handoffs & opportunities

Journeys break at the handoffs. These are the points where responsibility moves between people or systems, and where a product team can make the biggest difference.

HandoffRiskOpportunity
Applicant → placementDetails captured during recruitment are re-checked or re-entered when the placement is created.Carry the candidate record straight into the placement, with nothing re-keyed.
GTO → support providerTwo separate systems. Apprentice and employer details are entered again for the sign-up.Explore a data handoff between the platforms to cut duplicate entry and contract returns.
Government → bothRegistration status lives in the government system; the GTO may only learn about changes later.Surface registration status and milestones to the GTO's field officer.
Two sets of check-insThe field officer and the support provider both contact the apprentice, sometimes close together.Share a visit and contact timeline so check-ins complement each other.
💡
A product insight: each platform serves its own organisation well. The biggest remaining gains for the apprentice are in the space between them.

The apprentice journey, at a glance.

🚀
The takeaway: an apprenticeship is a multi-year journey shared by five parties and two platforms. Designing for the whole journey, especially the handoffs, is what turns good software into a good apprenticeship.
