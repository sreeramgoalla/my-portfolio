How I work

My approach to a complex initiative, from a loosely defined problem to a solution the delivery team can build with confidence.

  1. Frame the problemWho is affected, what outcome matters and what we don't know yet. Plan the investigation before any solution work.
  2. Understand usersInterviews, workshops and current-state observation across every user group, including those easiest to overlook.
  3. SynthesiseJourneys, pain points and rules, made visible so stakeholders share one picture of the problem.
  4. Shape optionsWith architects and engineers: feasibility, integration impact, effort and trade-offs, written up in plain language.
  5. SpecifyFunctional and non-functional requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria and business rules, each traceable to evidence.
  6. Support deliveryClarify intent through the build, refine the backlog, and keep scope and decisions visible.
  7. Validate & learnUAT, release validation and post-implementation reviews, fed back into the next round of discovery.
